Program 02

Air Drawing!

This is a program through which you could draw on a computer screen using your finger gestures in the air, and the camera would capture your finger gestures to register the color, stroke, et cetera. The idea behind this is really sophisticated. It was not an epiphany; however,a collection of small ideas which somehow took shape in my mind. Back when I was a kid, I listened to a keynote speech by Vinh Giang, which was beautiful. He performed a magic show in it, and I performed the same magic in my school assembly. There was a line in it: "Perspective is power, collaboration is power, and innovation is power, the moment you innovate outside your industry…" something like that, and it stayed with me. However, I am not an artist, my industry is computer science, technology, but I do have a wonderful artist in my life who inspired me to create such a beautiful program, to innovate outside my industry. And they say, the idea is worth far more than the output…!
